This platform was designed and developed by ITELLECT Private Limited Company as a comprehensive digital transformation case study (ITIPT02) for Mitsubishi Motors Cabanatuan.

Focused exclusively on the 2026 Mitsubishi model lineup at the Maharlika Highway, Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija branch, the web application unifies interactive vehicle discovery, real-time chattel mortgage financing estimation, factory after-sales PMS scheduling, and complimentary test drive reservations into a single cohesive experience.
